## Outline, pages 3–6 (Yan, 7 Oct)
Page 3: Ismahan at KAN Academy in 2040, mentored by Nusa. A large dashboard shows a blue river in a red zone: the Ayeyarwady at Myitsone. Nusa explains the anomaly: an ongoing war (below the donut's floor) while the ceiling expands nearby along the river, with a lot of PESA activity. She sends Ismahan to investigate.
Page 4: Soldiers clean up a riverine ecosystem; further up the river, another group of soldiers is cleaning it too. They spot each other and start shooting. This sets the context: the clashing armies fund their war by cleaning the river and drawing PESA funds for it. Ismahan meets representatives of the two sides. They have a long-running feud, but there is one person they both trust: "Oh look, here he is." Hamzah walks in; Ismahan is startled to see her brother. He is friends with leaders on both sides. He came to the war as a volunteer fighter, but quickly realised that care work is far more important than anything violence could accomplish, and got the armies to agree to take care of the natural environment and the communities around the river and leave them unharmed. This has led not just to sustaining the ecosystem but to a flourishing, symbiotic economy-ecosystem, with PROSPER creating wealth for the riverine communities. (It parallels how tiger conservation worked in Kachin State in the 1990s.) "Come hang out with me at the Riverkeepers Bar."
Page 5: The Riverkeepers Bar. Everyone loves Hamzah and offers to buy them both a drink. Over a round, the Riverkeepers explain what they do. Right at the end Faris comes in; Ismahan is surprised he is here as well.
Page 6: Faris explains what he has been up to. His new startup dismantles large hydropower dams while enabling communities to replace them with small hydropower projects. He is still brash and single-minded in his ambition, but now very passionate about the environment. He still looks at one number: his PROSPER stake. Ismahan ends the page with a sigh: Faris is still the same person, but at least his energy is now directed towards something good for everybody.
