## Through-lines
The line "Stop coming to me. Go to each other." Ismahan says it to Teja on page 4, and Teja opens the oration with it on page 5.
Kneeling: Ismahan kneels to Teja's height in the snow on page 4; on page 5 Teja kneels to Taro's.
Seeds that travel: the tin of Muara rice and the twist of fádnu on pages 1 and 4; the mangrove propagules floating out to sea in the last panel.
The teal: the aurora over the vault on page 1 becomes the teal of Taro's collar on page 5.

Her balloons take the collar's teal as an accent.

! CAPTION: …OPENED IN 2008 AS HUMANITY'S BACKUP OF LAST RESORT: MORE THAN A MILLION SAMPLES OF THE WORLD'S CROPS, FROZEN AT −18°C AGAINST WAR, DISASTER AND COLLAPSE.

### Panel 2.1 (cells 0–1)
Ismahan among the racks, talking; Teja listening.
! ISMAHAN (speech): When I was born, crisis was all around us: floods, fires, wars, crashes. The only way people knew to feel safe was to pile things up, and draw a line round what was theirs.
### Panel 2.2 (cells 2–3)
A montage in one panel: a bank vault door, a border fence, this vault's door.
! CAPTION: BANKS TO HOARD WEALTH. BORDERS TO HOARD LAND. BUNKERS TO HOARD THE LAST OF EVERYTHING, JUST IN CASE…
! CAPTION: …FROM THE PEACE OF WESTPHALIA (1648) TO THE SIX DISASTERS (2027), THIS WAS HOW THE MODERN WORLD KEPT ITSELF SAFE.
### Panel 2.3 (cells 4–5)
! TEJA (speech): So they were all selfish?
! ISMAHAN (speech): No, sayang. They were frightened. It's not the same thing. This whole mountain was a kindness to people they would never meet.
### Panel 2.4 (cells 6–7)
Biret tips a seed from her own pouch into Teja's palm.
! BIRET (speech): Here. Feel the shell.
! ISMAHAN (speech): That's the seed coat.
### Panel 2.5 (cells 8–11), wide strip
Close on the seed in Teja's open hand.
! ISMAHAN (speech, off panel): It's hard so the seed can sleep through the winter without drying out, or rotting, or being eaten.
! ISMAHAN (speech, joined to the last): Modernity was our seed coat, Teja. Banks and borders and bunkers kept humanity alive through a dangerous age.
! TEJA (speech): Then why break it?
! ISMAHAN (speech): Because a seed that never cracks its coat never grows. The coat isn't the enemy. It just isn't the plant.
! CAPTION: NOT AN EVIL PAST, BUT A PHASE: A HARD SHELL THAT HUMANITY HAD TO GROW INSIDE, AND THEN GROW OUT OF.
### Panel 2.6 (cells 12–13)
Biret pours from the thermos, unimpressed.
! BIRET (speech): You southerners call it a phase. To my people it was weather. The borders cut our reindeer pastures between four countries, and we waited it out.
### Panel 2.7 (cells 14–15)
Biret taps one rack.
! BIRET (speech): The first time seeds ever left this mountain, it was because of a war. In the old world, the archive was for after the end.
! CAPTION: IN 2015, CIVIL WAR DROVE THE INTERNATIONAL CENTER FOR AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH IN THE DRY AREAS (ICARDA) OUT OF ALEPPO. IT REBUILT ITS GENEBANK IN LEBANON AND MOROCCO FROM THE SEEDS IT HAD STORED HERE.

## Notes for checking
The Arendt quotation (6.1) is checked (5 Oct 2026): the original reads "men, though they must die, are not born in order to die but in order to begin", at the end of chapter V (Action), section 34, around p. 246 of the University of Chicago Press edition. The quoted words match exactly, with no comma. Her "men" is rendered as "humans" outside the quotation marks.
Fádnu as the Northern Sámi word for angelica, and chewing the stalks on migrations: check with a Sámi source before lettering.
Burial customs (5.1): Muara's are Malay and likely Muslim. Keep the scene simple, and check it.
Real facts, worth a final check: the vault (2008, 78°N, −18°C, over a million samples, depositors own their boxes); Svalbard's lack of an Indigenous population; Sápmi divided between Norway, Sweden, Finland and Russia; ICARDA's 2015 withdrawal and rebuilding in Lebanon and Morocco; Bretton Woods (July 1944, 730 delegates, 44 nations); the Nixon shock (15 August 1971); Tuvalu's 2023 amendment; the Zomia scholars; how long mangrove propagules stay viable at sea.
"Muara was the first to sign the Great Confluence Accord" (3.6) is new: canon says Sultan Nuh signs it in 2030, not that Muara was first. It follows Yan's note that Ismahan led Muara as the first polity to embrace the new world.
