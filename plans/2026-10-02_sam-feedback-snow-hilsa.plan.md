# Sam's feedback: teach the world through *The Year It Snowed* and *Visa for a Hilsa*

## Context

Sam reviewed the two story comics (relayed by Yan, 2026-10-02):

1. **The Year It Snowed (Ismahan and Ya):** visitors should learn the world's concepts as they
   read. The Act 2 scenes should show how the GDI pyramid has changed people's relationship with
   food. They should also show that the GDI is **one indicator among many**, where today GDP rules
   alone, and that **food policy in the Pluriverse is driven by the GDI**. Ya should grow from a
   provincial outlook into the person who becomes a researcher.
2. **Visa for a Hilsa:** don't leave Shapla's reluctance to the end. Add a **prologue page on
   forced migration**: generations of her women (variations on her look) through Partition, the
   1971 war of independence, the Ganges delta turned hostile by sea-level rise, and finally the
   village on the Meghna. End it with **displaced animals** (forest fire, dams, oil spills), which
   leads into the hilsa's POV on the next page.
3. **Visa for a Hilsa:** bring back ideas from Marcus and Shakil's original draft (the
   **Monsoon Hilsa** tab, non-canon until now), especially in **caption boxes on large panels**.
   Keep it centred on Jalal, but not at the expense of the ideas.

Inputs:

  4 pages on the old 3 × 4 grid.

## Goal

Revised scripts for both stories are approved by Yan (and Sam), live in the Edited Drafts script
tabs, and the two comics are rebuilt from them on A1 boards and checked page by page.

## Approach

- **Draft locally first** (`content/drafts/`), because the Google Doc is shared canon and image
  generation is costly. Push to the doc and rebuild only after Yan approves.
- **One page per scene**, as decided for the A1 rebuild:
  - *Snow* goes from 4 pages to 5: Act 1, two Act 2 scenes, two Act 3 scenes.
  - *Hilsa* goes from 4 to 7: the new prologue plus six scenes.
- **Concepts go in captions on the big panels.** Dialogue stays character-driven.
- **Snow, plural indicators:** each landing keeps a public board of many indices. The GDI is the
  one you can taste; Mua counts mudskippers. Ismahan's brother the Financier, who trusted one
  number, stands in for the GDP world.
- **Snow, policy:** a weakest-link score means help and budgets go to the weakest tier, ahead of
  need.
- **Snow, Ya's arc:** first time off the Ping → pockets the napkin pyramid → keeps a notebook →
  wants to *read* rivers, not only taste them. This bridges to Act Five.
- **Hilsa, Shapla's thread:** a **brass pot** carried by each generation. It goes on the shelf at
  the end of the prologue, stands behind her in every later scene, and is in her lap on the
  departing boat. Her reluctance is visible from page 4 on.
- **Hilsa, Monsoon ideas used:**
  - the Passage Covenant (the river holds fish in passage, and each place earns a share);
  - the NeurOtolith tag, nursery restoration and harvest abstention;
  - the dormant corridor, last transited 62 years ago, which matches Jalal's grandfather;
  - "custodial origin unresolved" as the real reason for 0 taka;
  - the Cooperative's fisher-co-op roots;
  - "one season is weather, three seasons is migration";
  - hilsa as border-free;
  - migratory "schools" of people, from the global-schooling idea.
- **Monsoon ideas left out:** the upstream/downstream custody dispute, the "emergent passage zone"
  capture story and the Dhaka chef. Each needs its own scenes and would pull focus from Jalal.
  They could seed another story.
