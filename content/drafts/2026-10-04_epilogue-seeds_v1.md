# Epilogue: Seeds — script v1

Replaces the flood story of *Moving House* with Yan's rework (see the
[plan](../../plans/2026-10-04_epilogue-seeds.plan.md)). Built from Khairun's ideas, with her
blessing. Svalbard, 2075, then Muara, 2090. Five A1 boards, 34 panels.

**Proposals, not yet decided** (marked ◇ where they appear):
- The Sámi elder is called **Biret**, and is on Svalbard because Sámi councils took on the vault
  when it passed into common keeping.
- The swap: Ismahan brings Muara rice; Biret gives *fádnu* (angelica) seed.
- Soraya and Ya are away on KAN field postings in 2075, which is why Ismahan takes Teja north.
- The funeral is in 2090, with Ismahan dying at 80.

## Board 1: The Bunker (2075)

### 1.1 The vault
Splash. Polar night on Svalbard; aurora over the mountain; the vault's concrete wedge in the snow.
Two small figures climb towards it: Ismahan (65), and Teja (11) carrying a tin.
!CAPTION: SVALBARD, 2075. THE SEED VAULT HAS STOOD IN THE PERMAFROST FOR SIXTY-SEVEN YEARS.
!CAPTION: ISMAHAN, 65, HAS BROUGHT HER GRANDDAUGHTER TEJA, 11, TO SWAP SEEDS, AND TO LEARN SOME HISTORY.

### 1.2 At the door
Teja looks up at the blank concrete front.
!TEJA: Why does it look like a bunker?
!ISMAHAN: Because it is one.

### 1.3 Biret ◇
The door opens. Biret, a Sámi elder in a work parka with a woven gákti band at the collar, a ring of
keys, a thermos.
!BIRET: You're late, Ismahan. Not that the seeds are going anywhere. That's rather the point.
!CAPTION: NOBODY IS FROM SVALBARD. WHEN THE VAULT PASSED INTO COMMON KEEPING, SÁMI COUNCILS TOOK IT ON: PEOPLE WHO KNOW HOW TO TEND A PLACE WITHOUT OWNING IT.

### 1.4 The tunnel
The long tunnel into the mountain; a painted line on the wall marks old meltwater.
!CAPTION: ONCE, MELTWATER FLOODED THIS TUNNEL. THE PERMAFROST THAT WAS MEANT TO KEEP IT COLD FOR FREE NEEDED HELP.

### 1.5 The racks
The cold room: shelves of sealed black boxes, each labelled with a country. Teja's breath fogs.
!ISMAHAN: More than a million seeds. Every country sent its own, sealed in boxes nobody else could open.
!TEJA: Like a bank.
!ISMAHAN: Exactly like a bank.

## Board 2: The Seed Coat

### 2.1 What they were afraid of
Ismahan among the racks, talking; Teja listening.
!ISMAHAN: When I was born, crisis was all around. The only way to feel safe was to pile things up, and draw a line round what was yours.

### 2.2 Banks, borders, bunkers
Three-part montage in one panel: a bank vault door, a border fence, this vault's door.
!CAPTION: BANKS. BORDERS. BUNKERS.

### 2.3 Not selfish
!TEJA: So they were selfish?
!ISMAHAN: They were frightened. It's not the same thing.

### 2.4 The shell
Biret tips a seed from her own pouch into Teja's palm.
!BIRET: Feel the shell.
!ISMAHAN: That's the seed coat. It keeps the seed alive through the winter.

### 2.5 The phase
Close on the seed in Teja's hand.
!ISMAHAN: Modernity was our seed coat. It kept humanity alive through a dangerous age.
!TEJA: Then why break it?
!ISMAHAN: A seed that never cracks its coat never grows.

### 2.6 Weather
Biret pours from the thermos, unimpressed.
!BIRET: You call it a phase. To us it was weather. We waited it out.

### 2.7 The only withdrawal
Biret taps one rack.
!BIRET: In all these years, seeds left this mountain once.
!CAPTION: WHEN WAR DROVE A SEED BANK OUT OF ALEPPO IN 2015, IT REBUILT ITS COLLECTION FROM THESE RACKS.
!BIRET: In the old world, the archive was for after the end.

## Board 3: A Day, and Decades

### 3.1 Money in a day
The vault's small warm office; cups steaming. Ismahan sketches on Teja's e-paper.
!ISMAHAN: A war built a whole new money system in three weeks. Another man ended it in a single day.
!CAPTION: BRETTON WOODS, 1944. THE NIXON SHOCK, 1971.

### 3.2 2027
Inset callback to the Prologue's six disasters.
!ISMAHAN: Money can be remade fast. After the six disasters, PLURIVERS remade it.

### 3.3 What didn't change
!ISMAHAN: But the borders stayed. The states stayed. The visas stayed.

### 3.4 An island with no land
A flag on a pole above open water.
!ISMAHAN: Can you have a country with no land? In 2023 Tuvalu wrote into its law that you can.

### 3.5 Valley, hill, mountain
Terraced hills north of the Ping; three bands of people by altitude.
!ISMAHAN: In the hills north of Ya's river, nobody asked "which country?" They asked: "valley, hill or mountain?"

### 3.6 Muara went first
Inset: Ismahan at 20 at the Confluence Accord (callback to *The Youngest Delegate*).
!ISMAHAN: Muara went first. It took everyone else decades to follow.

## Board 4: Go to Each Other

### 4.1 The swap ◇
At the racks again. Ismahan hands over a tin of Muara rice; Biret gives Teja a paper twist of seed.
!BIRET: Fádnu. Angelica. My grandmother chewed the stalks.
!TEJA: Will it grow in Muara?
!BIRET: No. Give it to someone who lives somewhere cold.

### 4.2 The couple on the Meghna
Inset callback to *Visa for a Hilsa*'s last panel: Jalal and Shapla's boat following the hilsa.
!ISMAHAN: Your mother's partner and my dear friend, Ya, once told me about a couple on the Meghna…

### 4.3 Chosen
!ISMAHAN: Her family had been forced to leave again and again. Then one day she chose to go.
!TEJA: Why?
!ISMAHAN: Because the fish knew the way.

### 4.4 The vote
!ISMAHAN: That's what Muara is going to do. The councils have voted. The Matriarchy ends with me.

### 4.5 The inheritance
Teja, small in the cold room, clutching the twist of seed.
!TEJA: But… what will I inherit?
!ISMAHAN: Nothing to hold. Everything to plant.

### 4.6 The line
Ismahan kneels to Teja's height.
!ISMAHAN: For fifty years, people have come to me. Now I'm telling them:
!ISMAHAN: Stop coming to me. Go to each other.

### 4.7 Out into the night
Leaving the vault under the aurora; Biret's door light behind them.
!TEJA: Will PLURIVERS dissolve one day too?
!ISMAHAN: I hope so. That's how we'll know it worked.

## Board 5: Muara (2090)

### 5.1 The gathering ◇
Wide: a simple burial on the bank above the mangroves. The crowd has come from everywhere: KAN
boats, a hydrofoil, bicycles, people in the dress of many rivers.
!CAPTION: MUARA, 2090. FIFTEEN YEARS LATER.

### 5.2 Soraya and Ya
Wordless. Soraya (51) and Ya (58), older, hand in hand among the mourners. Taro (38), the grey
tabby from the sailabout, now a beaing, lies wrapped round Ya's shoulders, her cybernetics plain
to see (see notes).

### 5.3 Teja speaks
Teja (26) at the water's edge, facing the crowd.
!TEJA: My grandmother's last words to most of you were the same. "Stop coming to me. Go to each other."

### 5.4 You did
!TEJA: You went. And look: today you've come to each other.

### 5.5 No title
!TEJA: I have no title to give up. She saw to that. So I can only offer this, and nobody has to take it.

### 5.6 What we are
!TEJA: From today, Muara is not who we are. It is what we are.

### 5.7 Two waters
!TEJA: Wherever a river meets the sea, wherever two waters mix, that is muara. And whoever lives there, we are their people.

### 5.8 Taro remembers
After the oration. Teja kneels by Ya; Taro looks up at her. Taro's balloons are her own style ◇.
!TEJA: Do you remember her, Taro?
!TARO: Every moment.
!TARO: She burnt the sesame balls, and laughed harder than Ya did.

### 5.9 Propagules
Wordless but for the caption. Mangrove propagules, already sprouted on the parent tree, drop into
the tide and float out to sea.
!CAPTION: muara (n.): the mouth of a river, where fresh water meets salt.

## Notes for checking

- *Fádnu* as the Northern Sámi word for angelica, and its traditional use: check with a Sámi source
  before lettering.
- Burial customs for 5.1: Muara's are Malay and likely Muslim; keep the scene simple and check it.
- The vault opened in 2008 (sixty-seven years by 2075). The Aleppo withdrawal (ICARDA, 2015) and
  Tuvalu's 2023 constitutional amendment are real.
- **Taro becomes a beaing** (Yan, 2026-10-05): a real biological cat until her late teens, then
  cybernetically enhanced into a *beaing*, Seapunk's *Southbeast Asia* term for an embodied AI, and
  a lifelong companion. The year, ~2070 (Taro about 18), is a proposal. She keeps all her
  biological memories, so she remembers 2052 in full, as a cat lived it (5.8). Before the
  enhancement she could not speak. Her voice follows
  the cat in *A Life Worth Watching* ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D67OU_Tvz8s)): dry,
  protective, never more words than needed. Sam's essay *A SEA of Distributed AI*
  ([Notion](https://seapunkstudios.notion.site/dai)) has the beaing and Airloom ideas; it calls SBA
  "a (weakly-held) strong vision", not binding canon.
- **Taro's look as a beaing: obviously cybernetic** (Yan, 2026-10-05). The 2052 and 2065 sheets
  stand, since she was enhanced later. Proposed design for a new cast sheet, *Taro at 38* ◇:
  - still recognisably the grey tabby, fur greyed at the muzzle with age;
  - one eye replaced by a round teal lens with a fine aperture ring; the other eye her own;
  - a line of small segmented plates down her spine, in brushed silver with ochre seams;
  - a slim collar band with a speaker grille at the throat, which glows teal when she talks;
  - small sensor tips on her ears.

  Her speech balloons take the collar's teal as an accent, so they read as hers.
- 4.7 and the river-as-a-legal-person idea (in the plan) are optional; cut 4.7 if Board 4 is full.
