## Why this revision

Sam's feedback (2 Oct 2026, relayed by Yan): a visitor who reads only this comic should come away understanding how the Pluriverse thinks about food. In Version 1 the Gross Delish Index was little more than a name. Sam asked for four things, and this is where each one lands.

1. How the GDI pyramid changed people's relationship with food: flavour is a health report on the ecosystem behind the dish. The napkin panel (2.4) teaches the five tiers; the floodplain (3.2) shows farmers and cooks living by them, and the land and its people both doing better for it.
2. The GDI is one indicator among many, where today GDP rules alone: the landing's board of many indices (2.1), Muara's mudskipper count and the Financier who trusted one number (3.3).
3. Food policy is driven by the GDI: the score is a weakest link, so help goes to the weakest tier first, ahead of need (2.4).
4. Ya grows from a provincial outlook into a researcher: "never this far from her hometown" (1.1), the board she never read (2.1), the napkin (2.4), the notebook (3.4), reading the board at home (4.1), and the last caption, which points to Act Five.

## Through-lines

The deck, seen from the same camera: on page 1 the two women stand at opposite ends, with luggage between them; on page 2 the napkin lies between them; on page 3 they sit side by side, legs dangling.
An object that travels: the napkin pyramid is drawn (2.4), folded into Ya's apron pocket (2.4), tucked into her notebook (3.4) and open on the rail at dawn (5.4).
Boards: the landing's board of many indices (2.1), whose counts come alive on the floodplain: children swimming, fireflies at dusk (3.2); the board by Dew's door that Ya never read, which she reads now (4.1); the question in her notebook, "What does the Ping count?" (3.4).
Two kinds of memory: Ya's 2040 is drawn in warm tones (4.3); Ismahan's 2028 in muted greys and cream (5.2).
The river level: it drops below the stilts (4.5) and exposes the riverbed (5.1).

## Lettering and prompt rules (from the playbook)

No words in the art. The board, the napkin and the notebook are drawn with blank chalk rows, bare bands and ruled lines; every word and number on them is lettered as a label box.
Prompts describe only the scene. Room for lettering comes from named scenery (a broad pale sky, a lantern-lit night sky, a snowy white sky), never from "leave space".
Dew's avatar is a figure in flat pale teal outlined in thin teal ink. Don't write "glow": it is on the style's avoid list.
On two-cell panels the faces fill the frame. Their balloons go across the border into the clear sky or deck of the panel above.

### Panel 2.1 (cells 0–3), wide strip

A riverside village landing. A long public chalkboard stands by the steps, with four blank chalk rows of tallies and arrows. Villagers swap seed jars with Ya; Ismahan stands beside her, and Ya stares up at the board.
! CAPTION: Once, one number ruled: GDP. Now every community tracks wellbeing by measuring many metrics, with both science and spirit. The Gross Delish Index is the one metric you can taste.
! LABELS (on the board's rows): GDI 71 · RIVER RICHNESS ↑ · FIREFLIES ON THE BEND · CHILDREN WHO SWIM
! YA (speech): Oh that thing! Ours hung by the door. I never read it.

### Panel 2.2 (cells 4–5)

On deck, Ismahan rolls sesame balls, badly. Flour everywhere.

### Panel 2.3 (cells 6–7)

A tray of burnt, lopsided sesame balls, smoke curling. Ya doubles over laughing.
! ISMAHAN (speech): In theory, a perfect seed commons.
! YA (speech): In practice, charcoal!

### Panel 2.4 (cells 8–15), large: the teaching panel

Evening on deck, under a dark lantern-lit sky. Between the two women, a napkin with a five-band pyramid sketched on it, the bands blank. Behind them, the landing's board is lit by lanterns. Taro bats a corner of the napkin. In a corner of the panel, Ya folds the napkin into her apron pocket.
! LABELS (on the napkin's bands, bottom to top): SOIL · WATER · SEED · AIR · WIND
! CAPTION: Flavour is a health report: each tier scores the ecosystem behind the food.
! CAPTION: The weakest tier sets the whole score, and money can't buy it back.
! CAPTION: So help goes to the weakest tier first, ahead of need.
! YA (speech): Grandma swapped seeds with every neighbour on the soi…
! ISMAHAN (speech): Then she was doing the GDI before it had a name.

### Panel 3.1 (cells 0–3), wide strip

Golden hour on a mudflat at the mangroves' edge. Ismahan crouches barefoot among mudskippers, muddy to the shins, a girl again. Ya sits on the edge of her moored sailboat, sharing fermented fruit with Taro, and smiles in spite of herself.
! ISMAHAN (speech): I grew up with these! I was always in the mangroves.
! YA (thought): So the princess was a mud kid too.

### Panel 3.2 (cells 4–11), large: the floodplain

Ismahan and Ya, small in the foreground and seen from behind, stand on the mudflat's bank with mudskippers at their feet. They look out over a wide floodplain at dusk under a broad pale-gold sky. Each thing on it is small and clear: flooded rice paddies with fish in the water and ducks among the stalks; floating vegetable gardens; a row of beehives and fruit trees along the mangrove fringe; stilt houses with ferment jars on the porches; a bamboo fish weir with a wide gap left open; children swimming in a clear channel; fireflies starting over the water. At a jetty, a farmer loads baskets of rice and fruit onto a market barge, and a cook in an apron checks a handful of grain.
! CAPTION: Beyond the mangroves, a floodplain farmed for the GDI: the river let in, and the fish, ducks and bees with it.
! CAPTION: Every tier they lift shows in the taste, and in what cooks will pay. As the plain thrives, so do its people.
! YA (speech): Even the rice tastes of the river here.

### Panel 3.3 (cells 12–13)

Dusk. The two sit side by side on the deck's edge, legs dangling over the mud, the fruit bowl between them. Mudskippers flick past below.
! ISMAHAN (speech): In Muara we count mudskippers. My brother the Financier counted only money.
! YA (speech): Grandma used to say a number's only as good as the tongue that checks it.

### Panel 3.4 (cells 14–15)

Night, lamplight. Ya writes in a notebook with blank ruled pages, the napkin pyramid tucked inside; Taro asleep on her lap.
! CAPTION: Ya's family came to the Ping river valley from China, generations ago. Home, she said, travels with you. That night she started a notebook.
! LABEL (on the notebook page): WHAT DOES THE PING COUNT?

## Inventions (approved by Yan, 2 Oct 2026)

These are not in Edited Drafts or Jules's material.
The landing's board of many indices: the GDI, the PROSPER river-richness index, and local counts (fireflies on the bend, children who swim, Muara's mudskippers).
"Help goes to the weakest tier first, ahead of need": Jules's weakest-link score plus PREMISE's motto.
Ya has never been this far from Chiang Mai, and never read the board by her own door.
The Financier line, which refers to The Youngest Delegate's 2029 crisis.
Ya's notebook, and the last caption: she soon leaves the kitchen for research. The canon says the sailabout inspired the move; "soon" sets no year.
Dew's line in the inset (5.2) quotes her line in The Youngest Delegate's panel 2.4.
The floodplain farmed by the GDI (3.2): fish in the rice, hives on the fringe, an open weir, and food that sells for more the healthier its tiers. The market barge and the rice boat at Dew's jetty (4.1) carry it along the chain to the cooks.
