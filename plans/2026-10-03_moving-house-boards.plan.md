# Moving House (the Epilogue) on A1 boards, from Khai's paneling

## Context

- Khairun's tab **RIVERBOOK › Khairun: Moving House** (`t.dqk19wo73c67`) has a prose story (Teja,
  the House of Taming, the flood that does not recede) and a paneling: four tables, one per arc beat —
  *Inheritance*, *The Flood*, *The Choice*, *The Succession* — whose merged cells give the panel
  layout. 27 panels in all.
- Yan, 2026-10-03: use Khai's paneling for the prologue (*Moving House*) and make it a comic.
- The [canon](../content/canon/riverbanks-canon.md) lists *Moving House* as the Epilogue, "in
  development", and Prologue as Sam's *Six Disasters, One Dream*. Yan now calls it the prologue;
  the header said PROLOGUE until Yan corrected it: it is the **Epilogue**, set in 2090.
- The tab predates the canon family decisions, so it is adapted to the timeline sheet (canon):
  - Teja b. 2064, so 26 in **2090** (matches Khai).
  - **Grandmother is Ismahan** (b. 2010, so 80). Khai's "Sultana Kandaria" was removed on
    2026-10-01. Ismahan founded the kinship councils (Khai gives that to a great-grandmother), and
    Sultan Nuh signed the Confluence Accord (Khai's "great-great-grandfather").
  - **The childhood scene is 2075, Teja at 11** — the sheet has "11, a scene with Grandma
    Ismahan" for 2075. Khai's prose says Teja was 6; his character sheet says "11sies".
  - **Mother is Soraya** (b. 2039, 36 in the flashback, 51 on her return), who left for the KAE Corps.
  - **Muar → Muara**, per canon decision 5.
  - **Jentayu** carries "the voices of the House's elders"; Khai's "great-grandmother, the last
    monarch" has no canon match (the last monarch was Sultan Nuh). After the ritual it carries
    Ismahan's voice too.

## Goal

A comic *Moving House* of four A1 boards in the house style, one per Khai table, with his merged
cells as the panels, in his reading order. Every panel carries his beat; the lettering comes from
his prose, at ≤ ~130 words a board; every board is checked by eye; recurring faces and sets come
from the style's cast.

## Approach

- **Khai's tables are the grids.** Board 1 is 4 rows × 6 columns (his table is 6 wide: a 2-2-2 row
  and a 3-3 row). Boards 2–4 are 5 rows × 2 columns.
- **Script first** in `content/drafts/2026-10-03_moving-house_v1.md`, then build. The script goes
  into a *Script: Moving House* tab only when Yan says so (it is not yet in Edited Drafts).
- **Infographic panels** (the ledger, the basin maps, the clause) carry no drawn text: labels and
  columns are lettered captions, as on PAO.
- **New cast:** Teja (26), Teja at 11, Ismahan at 80 and at 65, Soraya (51), Soraya at 36, Syahbandar,
  Maya the one-eyed mudskipper, Jentayu (the bird vessel), the House of Taming (place).
- **Through-lines:** the House seen from the same mangrove viewpoint on 1.7 and 4.6 (whole, then
  half drowned); Jentayu on 1.6 and 4.3; the "hold the House" line on 1.2, echoed on 3.4.
- **Considered and rejected:** Khai's Act I–III character arc (Maiden/Mother/Crone, elders dying)
  — the paneling and narrator arc supersede it; the finance-institutions table — reference only.
