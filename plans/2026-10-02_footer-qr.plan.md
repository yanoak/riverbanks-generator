# A QR code and link to riverbanks.lol in the board footer

## Context

The A1 board footer has three text slots, left, center and right. They default to
`RIVERBANKS · {page} · SEAPUNK STUDIOS`, and a page can override any of them (see
[the A1 board plan](2026-10-02_a1-board-format.plan.md) and `src/lib/model/bands.ts`).

Yan asked on 2026-10-02 for a link and a QR code to **riverbanks.lol** in the footer, so that
visitors walking past a board can reach the site. riverbanks.lol is already the exhibition's
address on Sam's title slide.

## Goal

Every board's footer shows, by default, a QR code at its right end that encodes
`https://riverbanks.lol`, with `RIVERBANKS.LOL` printed under it.

- **PDF:** both are a clickable link.
- **PNG:** they appear in the image.
- **Editing:** the address is a footer slot like the others: a comic-wide default, overridable
  per page, and blanked with `''`. It can be edited in the Inspector and through
  `set_header_footer`.

## Approach

- **The slot:** a new footer slot `qr` holds the address. A bare host gets `https://` in front
  when it is encoded and linked; the printed text shows the host in capitals.
  - The house default is `riverbanks.lol`.
  - A comic whose stored `bands` predate the slot falls back to the house value, because
    `bandSource` now fills missing slots from `HOUSE_BANDS`.
- **The code:** `qrcode-generator` (MIT, no dependencies) produces the module matrix, which
  `PageView` draws as one SVG path. It is vector, so it stays sharp at A1 and in print.
  - Error correction is **M**, enough for a board that may get scuffed.
  - Rejected: the `qrcode` package. It pulls in more dependencies, and its canvas and PNG
    output is not wanted.
- **Size:** the QR is 150 units square, plus its label, inside the 208-unit footer, on a white
  quiet zone. That is about 89 mm on A1. The usual rule of thumb is a scanning distance of about
  ten times the code's size, so it scans from about 0.9 m, close to where Sam's boards are
  read.
- **The link:** an `<a href>` around the QR and its label, so Chrome's print to PDF keeps a live
  link. In the editor the bands ignore pointer events, so it doesn't navigate.

   QR, and Reset to default brings it back.

4. In the print preview (PDF), the QR is a clickable link.

## Outcome

Done on 2026-10-02.

- **Every board footer** now ends in a 150-unit (about 89 mm) QR code, with RIVERBANKS.LOL
  under it. The left, centre and right text keeps its centring: the footer pads both sides by
  the QR's width.
- **Verified in Chrome:**
  - Chrome's `BarcodeDetector` decoded the rendered QR as `https://riverbanks.lol`.
  - Both print pages carry a live `<a href="https://riverbanks.lol/">`, and the editor canvas
    has none.
  - Clearing the "QR link" field on a page hides its QR, and Reset to default brings it back.
- **Not checked:** a phone scan of a printed board, and opening the PDF preview itself.
- **The link is print-only on purpose.** Thumbnails on the comics list sit inside a link
  already, so a nested one is avoided (`PageView`'s `linkable` prop).
