# Script: The Year It Snowed (v2 draft, 2026-10-02)

*Approved by Yan on 2026-10-02. It replaces the v1 script tab once written into Edited Drafts.*

## Why this revision: Sam's feedback (2026-10-02, relayed by Yan)

**The principle: readers learn the world as they read.** The exhibition doesn't only tell
character stories. It is how visitors meet the ideas of the Riverbanks world. A visitor who reads
this comic and nothing else should come away understanding how the Pluriverse thinks about food.
In v1 the Gross Delish Index was little more than a name: Ismahan says "Soil! The base of the
pyramid", sketches a napkin, and tells Ya her grandmother "was doing the GDI before it had a
name". The pyramid was there, but nothing explained it.

**What Sam asked for, in the Act 2 scenes:**

1. **Show how the GDI pyramid has changed people's relationship with food.** In the Pluriverse,
   flavour is a health report of the ecosystem behind the food. Eating is a way of reading the
   land, and that changes how people grow, swap, share and judge what they eat. The Act 2 scenes
   (Seed and Air) are where the two women trade skills and theory, so they are the natural place
   to show this.
2. **Show that the GDI is one indicator among many.** Today GDP reigns alone, and a single number
   stands in for a society's wellbeing. The Pluriverse keeps a plurality of indicators. The GDI
   matters, but it sits beside others: planetary ones such as the PROSPER richness index, and
   local ones that each community chooses for itself. The point is the contrast with a
   one-number world.
3. **Show that food policy in the Pluriverse is driven by the GDI.** The index is not just
   something to admire. It decides where effort and resources go. A weakest-link score sends help
   to the tier that is failing, and does so ahead of need, as PREMISE's motto says.
4. **Make Ya's growth visible.** At the start of the sailabout she is provincial: she knows food,
   the land and her stretch of the Ping deeply, but nothing beyond them, and she has never studied
   the GDI she has lived all her life. The story should show her outlook widening, until it sets
   her off on her career as a researcher. That career is where we meet her again in Act Five, *A
   Visa for a Hilsa*.

**How this draft answers it:**

- **The board of many indices** (page 2) is the GDP contrast.
- **The napkin panel's captions** (page 2) teach the GDI and the policy it drives.
- **Mua's mudskipper count and the Financier's "one number"** (page 3) make plurality personal.
- **Ya's arc** runs from "never this far from the Ping" to the napkin, the notebook, and the last
  caption pointing to Act Five.

**Format:** five A1 boards, one per Edited Drafts scene. Each board has a header band, a square
4 × 4 grid (cells 0–15, left to right, top to bottom) and a footer band. Balloons stay short;
concepts go in caption boxes on the large panels. Budget: 80–120 words a page.

**Header (every page):** ACT FOUR / THE YEAR IT SNOWED, with the page title below.

**Art style:** the house ligne claire style. Accent: brick red, only on Ya's apron.

**Character designs:** as in v1 (Ya at 20, Taro the kitten, Ismahan at 42, Grandma Dew, little
Ya), plus Ismahan at 18 for one inset on page 5.

## Page 1: Boatmates (Act 1, Soil)

**1.1 (cells 0–7), wide.** Morning on the Chao Phraya. Ya's small patched sailboat; a sleek
fancy boat pulls alongside.
- CAPTION: The Chao Phraya, 2052. Ya, twenty, is on a KAN sailabout. She has never been this far
  from the Ping.

**1.2 (cells 8–9).** Ismahan steps across with far too much luggage.
- ISMAHAN: I'm told we're boatmates.

**1.3 (cells 10–11).** Ya holds out a steaming bowl from the claypot.
- YA: Then eat. Taro pot. It's been going since dawn.

**1.4 (cells 12–15), wide.** They eat on deck. Ismahan is delighted, Ya puzzled. Ya slips a morsel
to the kitten.
- ISMAHAN: Soil! The base of the pyramid.
- YA: The base of the what?
- ISMAHAN: You've never seen a GDI board?
- YA: Back home we just *taste* things.

*≈ 60 words*

## Page 2: The Failed Sesame Balls (Act 2, Seed)

**2.1 (cells 0–7), wide.** A riverside village landing. A big public board stands by the steps, a
chalked dashboard of many indices side by side:
- **GDI 71, NICE**, broken into SOIL 80 · WATER 58 · SEED 77 · AIR 74 · WIND 69, with "WEIR VOTE
  THURSDAY" chalked beside WATER;
- **RIVER RICHNESS** (the PROSPER index) ↑;
- local counts: **FIREFLIES ON THE BEND**, **CHILDREN WHO CAN SWIM**.

Villagers swap seed jars with Ya. Ya stares up at the board.
- CAPTION: Once, one number ruled the world: GDP, money changing hands. Now every landing keeps a
  board of many.
- CAPTION: The Gross Delish Index is the one you can taste.
- YA: Our soi never had a board.

**2.2 (cells 8–9).** Ismahan rolls sesame balls, badly. Flour everywhere.
- ISMAHAN (thought): How hard can it be?

**2.3 (cells 10–11).** A tray of burnt, lopsided sesame balls, smoke curling. Ya doubles over.
- ISMAHAN: In theory, a perfect seed commons.
- YA: In practice, charcoal!

**2.4 (cells 12–15), wide, the teaching panel.** Evening on deck. Ismahan has sketched the
five-tier pyramid on a napkin (Soil, Water, Seed, Air, Wind) with a number beside each tier.
Behind them, the landing's board is lit by lanterns. The captions sit around the napkin like
labels:
- CAPTION: Flavour is a health report. Each tier scores the ecosystem behind the food.
- CAPTION: The score is a weakest link. One sick tier drags the dish down, and Pucks can't buy it
  back up.
- CAPTION: So help goes to the weakest tier first, ahead of need: seed banks, swap days, a weir
  reopened.
- YA: Grandma swapped seeds with every neighbour on the soi…
- ISMAHAN: Then she was doing the GDI before it had a name.

Silent beat in the same panel: Ya folds the napkin into her apron pocket.

*≈ 125 words*

## Page 3: Mudskippers (Act 2, Air)

**3.1 (cells 0–7), wide.** Golden hour on a mudflat. Ismahan crouches barefoot among mudskippers,
a girl again.
- ISMAHAN: I grew up with these! I was always in the mangroves.

**3.2 (cells 8–9).** Ya on the boat's edge, softening, shares fermented fruit with the kitten.
- YA (thought): So the princess was a mud kid too.

**3.3 (cells 10–11).** Ismahan counts on her fingers as mudskippers flick past.
- ISMAHAN: In Mua, we count mudskippers. They're on our board.
- YA: You count fish that walk?

**3.4 (cells 12–13), the Air panel.** Side by side on deck, legs dangling, the fruit bowl between
them. Behind them, the mangrove fringe is full of bees, birds and drifting spores.
- CAPTION: Air foods (fruit, honey, ferments) need pollinators and wild microbes that nobody
  controls. They score high only where the wild is left alone.
- ISMAHAN: My brother the Financier trusted one number. His friends could raise anything, except a
  harvest.
- YA: Grandma used to say a number's only as good as the tongue that checks it.

**3.5 (cells 14–15).** Night. Lamplight. Ya writes in a notebook: tier scores for every stop, the
napkin pyramid tucked inside. One page has a question: "What does the Ping count?"
- CAPTION: Upriver. Up the Ping. Towards Chiang Mai.

*≈ 105 words*

## Page 4: Peaberry Rice in Chiang Mai (Act 3, Scene 1)

**4.1 (cells 0–3), wide.** The family restaurant on stilts over the Ping. A small GDI board is
chalked by the door. Ya reads it now.
- CAPTION: Chiang Mai. The sailabout ends where Ya began.

**4.2 (cells 4–5).** Ya at the wok with peaberry rice. Ismahan hovers, about to touch something.
- YA: Grandma's peaberry rice. Don't touch anything.

**4.3 (cells 6–7).** Ya, still cooking, looks into the steam.
- YA: The year I was eight, it snowed in Chiang Mai.

**4.4 (cells 8–13), large flashback, warm tones.** 2040. Snow on Warorot's rooftops. Grandma Dew
holds a single pearly grain up for little Ya. A thin blue line of snowmelt runs from the hills
down the fai channels to the paddies.
- CAPTION: 2040.
- DEW: This grain walked further than you have, little duck.
- CAPTION: Snowmelt ran down the shared fai to the paddies. Peaberry rice grows where Water meets
  Seed.

**4.5 (cell 14).** Present. Ismahan, moved.
- ISMAHAN: I feel I know her already.

**4.6 (cell 15).** Ya at the window: the river has dropped below the stilts.
- YA: The water's gone down. Come, I'll show you her shrine.

*≈ 90 words*

## Page 5: The Shrine (Act 3, Scene 2)

**5.1 (cells 0–7), wide.** Under the stilts, a small shrine on the exposed riverbed. Dew's digital
avatar glows above it. Ismahan freezes.
- CAPTION: Dew's shrine can be reached only when the river lets you.

**5.2 (cells 8–9), inset in grey tones.** The Sunda Summit flotel, 2028, drawn as in Sam's slides 5–6: a floating city of pontoons around an arched Convention Hall, not a ship. Ismahan at 18 shares a
meal with an older Thai woman: Dew.
- ISMAHAN: I know her. The Sunda Summit. I never learned her name.

**5.3 (cells 10–11).** The two women kneel by the shrine.
- ISMAHAN: She taught me that food ties everything together: everything it comes from, not just
  us. I took that home to Mua.
- YA: …She taught me too.

**5.4 (cells 12–15), wide.** Dawn. Ya stands on the restaurant deck with her notebook open,
looking downriver past the bend. Taro is on the rail.
- CAPTION: Ya had tasted the river all her life. Now she wanted to read it, and every river
  beyond it.
- CAPTION: She would soon leave the kitchen for research. → Act Five: A Visa for a Hilsa

*≈ 85 words*

## Inventions to confirm

Things not in Edited Drafts or Jules's material. Sam's feedback asks for the first three in
spirit, but the specifics are mine.

1. **The landing's board of many indices.** It shows the GDI, the PROSPER river-richness index,
   and invented local counts: fireflies on the bend, children who can swim, and mudskippers in
   Mua. Swap in Sam's own indicators if he has a list. (His Monsoon Hilsa note about a "hilsa
   index as sovereign wealth indicator" could appear on a board in the Hilsa comic.)
2. **"Help goes to the weakest tier first, ahead of need."** This combines the weakest-link score
   (Jules) with PREMISE's motto. The "WEIR VOTE THURSDAY" detail is from Jules's Fai-Rice strip.
3. **Ya has never been this far from the Ping.** It sets up her provincial starting point.
4. **The Financier line.** It refers to *The Youngest Delegate*'s 2029 crisis, where his Wall
   Street friends couldn't help.
5. **Ya's notebook, and the final caption** saying she soon leaves the kitchen for research. The
   canon says the sailabout inspired the move; "soon" sets no year.
